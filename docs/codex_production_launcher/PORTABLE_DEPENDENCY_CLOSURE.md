and support the explicitly attested local-host adoption mode.  They are not
used by collaborator STATIC, PREFLIGHT, PHYSICAL, or PRODUCTION.  Historical

dereferenced by the collaborator workflow.

do not define a portable worker count. The active authority is a receipt issued
on the destination host for exactly the requested `Workers=N`.
